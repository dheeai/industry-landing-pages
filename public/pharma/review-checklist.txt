DHEE STUDIO
Scientific video review checklist

This checklist helps prepare a review. It is not a substitute for your organization's Medical, Legal, Regulatory, scientific, or compliance review and does not confer approval.

BEFORE STORYBOARD
[ ] Identify the intended audience, use, and channel.
[ ] Provide the approved source references and version/date for each.
[ ] Name the scientific and claim owners who can resolve questions.
[ ] Separate established evidence from assumptions or planned data.
[ ] Confirm whether the content is scientific education, promotional, patient-facing, or internal.
[ ] Agree data-handling requirements before sharing confidential material.

BEFORE VISUAL DEVELOPMENT
[ ] Confirm the mechanism, target, ligand, pathway, and biological context with a subject-matter expert.
[ ] Confirm what is known, simplified, omitted, or shown schematically.
[ ] Check that scale, cell type, molecular structure, sequence, and labels are not misleading.
[ ] Review the script, storyboard, and visual direction before full production.
[ ] Identify any client-specific AI disclosure or provenance requirements.

BEFORE FINAL HANDOFF
[ ] Review scientific statements against the approved references.
[ ] Check claims, balance, intended audience, and applicable product context.
[ ] Review captions, terminology, pronunciation, translations, and accessibility.
[ ] Check that all visual simplifications are acceptable and disclosed where needed.
[ ] Confirm version labels, approved formats, ownership, and permitted use.
[ ] Record client review status and final sign-off through the client's process.

REMINDER
DHEE provides managed AI-assisted production and human creative QA. DHEE does not provide clinical evidence or replace client scientific, Medical, Legal, Regulatory, or compliance review.
